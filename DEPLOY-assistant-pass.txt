TALE OF TWO — assistant pass (v13.1.27)

Upload these to the website (GitHub / studio.taleoftwoweddings.com):

  index.html
  assistant-pass.html

Then publish database.rules.json in Firebase Console → Realtime Database → Rules.
Without that, the Assistant pass button cannot save.

How to send it
  Live day or Team & prep → Assistant pass
  WhatsApp opens with client name, arrival time, location, and a no-login link.

The link works inside WhatsApp. Assistants do not sign in.
It shows only briefing details — not crew, pay, or check-in.
