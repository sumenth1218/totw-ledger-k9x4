WHAT IS NEW:
- Each event now has explicit Candid and Traditional coverage controls in Weddings → Team & prep.
- Marking a team Not required clears false CP/CV or TP/TV shortages without removing assigned crew.
- New events no longer assume CP, CV, TP and TV are all required before you choose or assign the team.
- A confirmed booking appears immediately as a Weddings folder, even before its private client link exists.
- Opening that new folder prefills the client-link setup from the booked dates and event names.
- Creating/updating the client link refreshes the folder immediately.
- Saving a published crew assignment writes to the existing Crew App assignment path and appears in an already-open Crew App without a manual reload.
- The existing Copy crew link and WhatsApp crew actions remain available after the client event is approved/published.
- Clients are shown one wedding month at a time and sorted by their first event in that month.
- Weddings spanning more than one month appear in every relevant month.
- The global Deliverables tab shows every client grouped and ordered by the date of their final event.
- Client-link details remain the source for events, dates, timings, venue, Maps and contacts.
- Exact Crew Planner rows automatically supply registered team members and CP/CV/TP/TV roles.
- Before publication, a Planner team change updates the internal draft automatically and asks you to save it.
- After publication, a Planner team change is held for review and never replaces the live assignment silently.
- Once any crew member checks in, that event's timings and assignments are locked.
- Combined Planner rows require one explicit decision before applying their team to separate client events.
- Unregistered or ambiguous Planner names are shown clearly and never silently omitted.
- Manual team changes remain available as a collapsed event-only adjustment.
- Pending client approval still allows internal planning; crew publishing remains locked until approval.
- Client details, advanced team controls, lists and boards, and Shoot Record stay collapsed until needed.
