NEW OPERATING MODEL
- Today is the owner command center for due follow-ups, client reviews, upcoming events and delivery work.
- Team is the single crew directory for identity, contact, default role and default day rate.
- Weddings → Team & prep is the only normal crew-assignment and day-cost editor.
- Day-of reads the same event record for check-in, checkout, reminders, cards and handover.
- Finance receives one crew cost per wedding/date/person, even when that person covers several events that day.
- TP/TV time remains reference only; price is always entered manually.
- New client event IDs are immutable; partial approvals and approved snapshots are preserved.
- Legacy standalone checklist URLs now send crew into the authenticated Crew App.
- Traditional Partners can be assigned as one outsourced unit per wedding date. The private cost belongs to the partner/day, while a temporary captain pass carries only operational details.
